About

I am an Information Science student at Michigan State University, with a passion for design. I have always been enraptured by the concept of sending a message through design, whether that be the Apple Ecosystem and its theme of an elegant, sleek future, or even the homey and welcoming design of an Applebee's. Both of those ideas are communicated expertly and covertly, akin to an art. I want to be that artist.

I have completed multiple internships throughout my academic career, two of which being coding training under Apple; we were tasked with designing and developing prototypes of deployable app ideas, while learning Apple's programming language, Swift. I am currently using this previous experience to develope a game on Roblox with several peers, learning LuaU along the way.

My goal— To create impactful designs that resonate with users and make a difference in their lives.
